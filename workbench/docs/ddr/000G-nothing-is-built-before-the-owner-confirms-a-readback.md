# Nothing is built before the owner confirms a readback

Nothing is mapped, planned or built until the owner confirms a readback naming the concept, direction, cost, reason and what will be created. A finished document or a positive reaction is not confirmation.

Considered and rejected: treating silence, a liked draft or a finished document as agreement.

Consequences: Agents present a readback before Map, Plan or Build and wait for the owner's confirmation.

Provenance: Owner decision review, 2026-11-01.
