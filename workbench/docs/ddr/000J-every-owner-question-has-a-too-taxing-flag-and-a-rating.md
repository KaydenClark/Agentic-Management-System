# Every owner question has a too-taxing flag and a rating

Every owner question has a one-click "too taxing" flag and a 1 to 5 rating. A flagged question is rewritten before it is asked again. Flags, ratings and comments are committed records the loop reads, never ignored local files.

Considered and rejected: keeping ratings in ignored local notes, where nothing can learn from them.

Consequences: The owner's ratings are input to the voice guide and the RSI loop.

Provenance: Owner decision review, 2026-11-01.
