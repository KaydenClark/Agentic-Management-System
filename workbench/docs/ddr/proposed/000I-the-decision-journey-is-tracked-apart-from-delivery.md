# The decision journey is tracked apart from delivery

The decision journey, from open question to confirmed decision on the Grill Board, is tracked apart from delivery, which Specs and Tasks track.

Considered and rejected: one tracker for both, which mixes unanswered questions with work in flight.

Consequences: The Grill Board shows decisions; the Taskboard shows delivery.

Provenance: Owner decision review, 2026-11-01.
