# The Blueprint is one short four-part page

BLUEPRINT.md is one short page in four parts: What it is, Who it serves, Promised outcomes, Non-goals. It is written fresh, links no record IDs, and maps each promised outcome to an eval task or labels it untested.

Considered and rejected: carrying over an older Blueprint's text, or a long page that mixes destination with procedure.

Consequences: An untested outcome is labeled as such until an eval task covers it.

Provenance: Owner decision review, 2026-11-01.
