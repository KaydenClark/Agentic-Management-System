# A Spec is accepted against its conditions

A Spec is accepted only against its stated conditions and the delivered result. "All Tasks done" is a coordination check, not acceptance.

Considered and rejected: accepting a Spec because its Tasks closed.

Consequences: Acceptance names each condition and its evidence.
