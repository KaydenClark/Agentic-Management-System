# The Wiki is a small linked synthesis

The Wiki is a small linked synthesis: tens of topic pages (start under about 30), each with sources, uncertainty and corrections, plus one index. No per-term or per-skill pages that duplicate their owners.

Considered and rejected: one page per term or skill, which duplicates the Glossary and the skills.

Consequences: New topics join an existing page before a new one is made.

Provenance: Owner decision review, 2026-11-01.
