# A confirmed Grill Board answer goes straight to its owner

A confirmed Grill Board answer is written straight into its owning record, with no bookkeeping step and no re-confirmation.

Considered and rejected: a separate apply or reconcile step that leaves confirmed answers waiting.

Consequences: The board records the answer; the agent applies it to the durable owner in the same change.

Provenance: Owner decision review, 2026-11-01.
