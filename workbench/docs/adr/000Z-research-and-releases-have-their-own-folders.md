# Research and releases have their own folders

The research lane holds maintained investigations, one folder per topic, with no private or licensed raw text in the public repository. A releases folder starts at the first release, one folder per version.

Considered and rejected: publishing raw research or private traces.

Consequences: Private evidence stays in a private repository.

Provenance: Owner decision review, 2026-11-01.
