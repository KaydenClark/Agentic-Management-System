# Capture a decision before the output that depends on it

A consequential decision or correction is captured on its owning page before the first output that depends on it.

This is a hypothesis. The owner confirmed it as a working rule, and the RSI loop tests whether it holds. Confirmed if over real work with real corrections, an unbriefed fresh recipient recovers the confirmed value, the superseded value, the open question, the owner of the work and the next action, with fewer owner re-explanations than a no-capture baseline. Refuted if the recipient misses corrections or needs re-explanation at the baseline rate.

Considered and rejected: capturing at the end of a session, after outputs already depend on the decision.

Consequences: Capture happens in the owning record, not in notes.
