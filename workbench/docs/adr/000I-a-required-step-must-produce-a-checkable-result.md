# A required step must produce a checkable result

A required step must produce a checkable result now. A step whose value is unproven is optional and becomes a hypothesis.

Considered and rejected: required ceremony justified by expected future value.

Consequences: Unproven steps are listed as hypotheses for the loop to test.
