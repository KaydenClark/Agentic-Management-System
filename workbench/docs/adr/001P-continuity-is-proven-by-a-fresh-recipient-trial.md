# Continuity is proven by a fresh-recipient trial

A fresh agent must recover the objective, the latest decision and its correction, the state and one next action from tracked records alone. Continuity is claimed only after a fresh-recipient trial shows it.

Considered and rejected: claiming continuity because notes exist.

Consequences: A cold-start eval task carries the trial.
