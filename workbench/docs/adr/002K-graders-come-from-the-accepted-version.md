# Graders come from the accepted version

The runner, graders and scoring procedure come from the accepted version, never from the candidate. A change to the evaluator is scored by the previous evaluator.

Considered and rejected: letting a candidate grade itself.

Consequences: Each row names the grader version, and a grader change starts a new series.

Provenance: Owner decision review, 2026-11-01.
