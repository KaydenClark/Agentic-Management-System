# Scaffolding is deleted at close

Specs, Tasks, handoffs and notes are scaffolding. At close, lasting knowledge moves to its owner and the scaffolding is deleted in the same change. What the site shows lives in Supabase, so deletion does not remove it.

Considered and rejected: keeping closed scaffolding as an archive agents must read around.

Consequences: Closing a Spec includes moving its lasting knowledge.

Provenance: Owner decision review, 2026-11-01.
