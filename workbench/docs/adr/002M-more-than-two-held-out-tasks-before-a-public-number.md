# More than two held-out tasks before a public number

No public rate is quoted from fewer than three held-out tasks. Until then the site reports results per task.

Considered and rejected: a public rate from two tasks.

Consequences: The held-out suite grows before the site quotes a rate.

Provenance: Owner decision review, 2026-11-01.
