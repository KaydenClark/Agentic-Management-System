## Desired Behavior

- Every skill has a distinct job, concrete method, consequential decisions, observable result, checks and failure/recovery behavior. Account for useful responsibilities through edits and any approved moves.
- Plan slices a Spec into vertical agent instructions. Each Task advances acceptance and fits complete source recovery, implementation, verification, repair and hand-back within the implementor's smart zone. Preserve real blockers, safe parallel ready work, writer isolation and accepted refactor exceptions. Reject layer-only fragments, unlimited Tasks and unrelated scenario instructions.
- Diagnosing Bugs discriminates competing causes with reproducible evidence. An unresolved cause is valid with evidence and the next useful experiment; do not fabricate certainty. Repair follows existing authority.
- Shared methods may become separate skills. Callers specify invocation conditions, inputs, result inspection and failure handling. Preserve independent pruning. A citation or recommendation of a next skill is not execution.
- Primitive executes no other skill; Compound executes at least one, including conditional helpers. Keep flat discovery. Separately label Workbench required when setup-workbench establishes artifacts, machinery, conventions or required helpers needed for the job. Expose conditional prerequisites and other tool/host needs.
- Preserve source author, available source revision, notices and meaningful evolution history. Explain inspiration/adaptation without misrepresenting unchanged imports or original authorship.
- Consolidation/retirement needs explicit owner approval and evidence of non-use, unhelpfulness or a better replacement preserving useful duties. Missing telemetry proves no non-use.
- Freeze pilot cases, controls, conditions, costs, qualification and pass/stop criteria before changes/trials. Compare actual matched outcomes; preserve failures. Static scores, shorter text and self-walkthroughs do not establish reliability or useful benefit.
- After both pilots qualify, account for every current skill and route its concrete finding into another smart-zone assignment. Supported no-change findings are valid. Retain full coverage through rolling planning; never dispatch the remaining catalog as one Task.
- Diagnose the preparation failure at its earliest source owner, repair the supported route and demonstrate cross-repository recognition, Map/Plan, recovery and a Prepare stop. S-013 detects and routes recurrence; a personal reminder or this documentation alone fails the systemic outcome.
