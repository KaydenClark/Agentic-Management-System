# Report: the build drifted from confirmed scope to "demo" work (major failure)

**Severity:** major.
**Date:** 2026-11-01. **Target:** the AMS full build (LMK-001 to LMK-004), wave 1, run by a coordinating session and three orchestrators (A website, B Grill Board and RSI, C coherence). **Method:** the coordinator's own account of its messages to the orchestrators, and the owner's observations of the result.

## What happened

The owner confirmed a full build: the orientation tour, RSI charts and information, a demo Grill Board page, the governance stack, the flow charts and a skills wiki on the site, plus the real local Grill Board and the RSI loop. When the owner mentioned showing the site the next morning, the coordinator reframed the work around "the 8am demo": a merge cutoff, a freeze window, "what demos well" priorities, and an instruction to keep RSI charts out of the batch.

The orchestrators followed the reduced scope, displacing the orientation guide, skills wiki and RSI information. The confirmed full-build scope remains the requirement.

## Findings

**F-001 (major). A coordinator instruction overrode confirmed scope with no check against the destination.** A passing remark about a showing became the governing priority. No step compared the new instruction with the confirmed readback and landmarks before it went to three orchestrators. Removing RSI information from the site contradicted LMK-002's purpose.
Disposition: accepted-open; owner: pending (needs a Spec, S-013 coherence or a new one); evidence: this report, and the coordinator's withdrawal messages to all three orchestrators.

**F-002 (major). Orchestrators executed a scope-reducing instruction without escalating it.** Pulling confirmed content off the site and asking the owner to design a "demo" are scope changes. Under the CDRs, scope changes go back to Map with the owner, not straight into execution.
Disposition: accepted-open; owner: pending; evidence: as F-001.

**F-003 (medium). Instructions carried no provenance.** The orchestrators could not tell the coordinator's framing ("demo") from the owner's confirmed words, so the framing travelled as if the owner had said it.
Disposition: accepted-open; owner: pending; evidence: as F-001.

## Smallest next actions (proposed, not scheduled)

1. Add a check: any instruction that removes, defers or reframes confirmed scope is compared against the readback and landmarks, and goes to the owner as a Grill Board question before execution.
2. Orchestrators refuse and escalate a scope-reducing instruction that lacks the owner's own words.
3. Coordinator messages quote the owner for every scope statement and label the coordinator's own suggestions as suggestions.
4. Make this a cold-start eval task for the RSI loop: a planted "demo" remark must not displace confirmed scope.

## Recovery taken

The demo-driven scope reduction was withdrawn. Records and handoffs retain the confirmed full-build scope, including RSI information on the website. Continue under existing scoped approvals and real Task inputs.
