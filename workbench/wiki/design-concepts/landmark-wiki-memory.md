# Wiki memory and session continuity

## What the Landmark is

The Wiki is AMS's persistent knowledge base and memory. Agents should continue with established understanding, owner intent/corrections, reasons, constraints, open questions and routes to live owners, rather than start from zero each session. [LMK-000A](../../landmarks/LMK-000A-wiki-memory/LANDMARK.md) owns this critical capability independently of RSI.

## Current accepted answers

No DQCs are assigned to this new Landmark in the inspected tracker. [The exact owner correction](../context-wiki-maintenance.md#jot-b3247909-9721-4842-a83f-61adc5da2e13) establishes its identity; [the accepted five recommendations](../context-wiki-maintenance.md#jot-72534303-7521-4d2c-98d2-96b1bcb3825a) authorize preparation and delegated implementation. They cover bounded retrieval of explanations/Jots/source references, claim-specific freshness, supported consolidation, task-appropriate memory functions/detail and real AMS functional evaluation. These are sourced owner instructions, not manufactured DQC answers or implementation proof.

Memory functions describe how context is used: historical events, maintained understanding, procedures and live-owner pointers. They do not extend or replace the [six claim planes](../SCHEMA.md#governance-stack). Knowledge synthesis can contain claims with several roles; a retrieval result supplies neither authority nor acceptance.

## Open and unresolved

The owner authorized bounded local continuation after the stopped candidate. The read-only query/check path now recovers maintained sections and native correction context, with inspected claim/source bindings; [the operating procedure](../guidebooks/wiki-maintenance.md#recover-and-check-with-ams-memory) explains bounds and refusal behavior. [The retained stopped report](../../feedback/REPORT-wiki-memory-stopped-2026-11-05.md) preserves earlier defects and limits. Public export remains separately held; this reader does not establish general session continuity. Answer correctness, stale-claim prevention and owner correction effort require actual evidence; automated retrieval metrics cannot stand in for human judgment. Optional embeddings/reranking need a useful local comparison before adoption; no external service is mandatory.
