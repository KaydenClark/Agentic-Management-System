# RSI Loop

## What the Landmark is

The RSI Loop connects observed failures, diagnosis, controlled repair, qualification, adoption and later use. It improves shared methods and the Workbench through evidence rather than treating a merged change, higher assessment score or operating worker as demonstrated benefit.

## Current accepted answers

The [current Map source](../context-feedback-improvement-loop-plan.md#jot-7862917b-48cb-41bc-8f21-0ddfc611cdac) carries the owner’s Servitor-first loop, optional participation, per-chat observer, Critical skipped requests, repair timing and one-active-repair rule. Repair uses author-skill and prune-artifact, tests draft and related skills in a separate real website session, then eligible merge automatically updates everyday skills. Supabase owns shared history and Git owns skills/tests.

Three audits produce distinct Quality and Regression percentages, Critical result and worst-failure grade. Whole-Workbench Alignment stays separate. Historical confirmed DQC-000N grade and DQC-000I requested-use decisions were inspected in the alignment branch; their source identities are preserved in the Map source. This update does not manufacture DQC confirmations or alter the other chat’s records.

## Open and unresolved

The existing scorer still uses the old passing-run-count grade. No Servitor observer or complete new improvement cycle was verified here. Plan selects the real failure, task, loading mechanism and fixtures and reconciles the earlier Stage A contracts against current Specs. Preserve attempts, frozen campaigns and holds. Map performs no implementation, installation, database writes, tests on Servitor or deployment. The destination is mapped; acceptance and recurrence benefit remain unproved.
