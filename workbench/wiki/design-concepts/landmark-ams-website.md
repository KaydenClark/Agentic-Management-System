# AMS website

## What the Landmark is

The website explains AMS to visitors and to the owner returning later. Orientation, skills summaries, governance diagrams, a sample Grill Board and RSI evidence make the working system understandable. The website is an output and evidence surface of AMS, not the whole reusable system.

## Current accepted answers

No DQCs are assigned to this Landmark in the inspected tracker; no card-confirmed answer is invented. The [Blueprint](../../../BLUEPRINT.md) and [Landmark direction](../../landmarks/LMK-003-ams-website/LANDMARK.md) supply existing intent: a Next.js presentation app in site/, Python backends, public-safe evidence and feature → dev → main delivery with owner-only dev-to-main promotion. The [owner purpose correction](../context-ams-purpose.md#jot-37abcff9-7be7-49a9-9ddb-98e281445cc8) distinguishes the reusable system from its website and bounded assessment.

## Open and unresolved

Inference: a successful source build alone cannot establish deployed-page usability or owner acceptance. Live deployment checks and page-specific evidence stay in their owners. This maintenance operation changes no website or deployment. The [real local Board](../context-grilling-board.md) is distinct from the sample demonstration; its unresolved redesign must not be hidden behind a demo or a green code check. [RSI presentation](landmark-rsi-presentation.md) must retain uncertainty and actual evidence strength.
