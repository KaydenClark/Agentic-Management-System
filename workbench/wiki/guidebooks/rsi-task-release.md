## Source repair and personal-use gate

S-011 history at the design base records setup-workbench/orchestrate/handoff merges with scoped qualification and journey's owner-directed no-regression merge, not a demonstrated fix of unreproduced failures. Land exhausted its three attempts; prune-artifact/writing-for-agents studied RUNBOOK evidence is development only; author-skill is held. Preserve every pin, review failure and attempt. Observed catalog source commit `804ba1ed3a8532b55fa0a7542945f806fb165883`; byte/source presence does not establish installation/host activation/later-use acceptance for any repair.

The assembler obtains exact case/qualification/source containment and authorized install/load/later-use receipts from their S-011 owner. Source merge releases no personal-use edge. TK-003 retains its TK-002 dependency. Held TK-002S depends natively on TK-002R and proves independent real website use, recurrence and safe dev readback when that old chain cannot supply the matching artifact. Reuse delivered subsets only at proven scope; they cannot close land or whole S-011.

If existing evidence cannot establish the representative source repair and adoption, use the [held TK-002R](../../landmarks/LMK-004-rsi-loop-during-build/specs/S-011-land-skill-events/tasks/TK-002R/TASK.md), with full source/campaign/adoption authority before execution. A supported no-source-change disposition is valid when diagnosis finds no skill cause; it does not establish the required repaired-source outcome. Otherwise start from the diagnosed earliest source owner, freeze the case/controls/grader before candidate work, preserve existing counters/stops, qualify the exact source under author-skill/source-repository checks and independent review, then verify authorized personal installation and a later native use. A new Task/version/campaign never resets or releases stopped work. If authorization or qualification is absent, keep this dependency open; do not substitute an AMS copy, fixture injection or remove personal-use acceptance.
